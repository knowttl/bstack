# Establish the vision

Produce a contribution acceptance policy the author can approve.
VISION.md explains which future changes fit the project and which reviewers should resist.
It must stand on its own without the board, transcript or a companion answers file.
Keep a backlog and implementation plan separate from the vision.

## Evidence and scope

Resolve the target, author and requested change before drafting.
For a new idea, confirmed author intent and decisions are the evidence.
Identify them as author decisions instead of claiming support from nonexistent history.
For an existing repo, read its approved vision, relevant design, code and accessible history.
Prefer merged pull requests where available, otherwise use real commit messages.
Never fabricate history or infer that every repeated pattern is an approved principle.
Separate documented intent, observed behaviour and inference, with citations.
If needed evidence is unavailable, preserve the draft and name the limitation.

An existing vision is the approved baseline.
An audit with no material vision gap does not open a vision interview or board.
When a gap or requested direction change warrants review, propose a reviewed delta with evidence newer than the baseline.
Never write a competing vision or silently replace the approved one.
Contradictions need an author decision before dependent recommendations.

## Draft an acceptance policy

Build a private evidence sheet mapping each commitment to a source or confirmed author decision.
Every drafted line must trace to that sheet or reasoning the author approved.
Generic virtues without a project-specific consequence do not establish a policy.
Use declarative present tense, concrete accept and resist criteria, and explicit non-goals.
Use one sentence per line and plain hyphens.

The draft contains:

- Why the project exists and who it serves.
- The single capability or responsibility it owns.
- Short principle sections with testable commitments, boundaries and refusals.
- Concrete non-goals.
- A closing pair of tests explaining when a change aligns and when it should be resisted.

Aim for a page or two, rather than a ledger of decisions or marketing copy.
In delta mode, keep the baseline and list candidate line edits independently, each with its evidence.
Keep the draft, evidence sheet, board and transcript in scratch outside the target.
Protected creation or replacement remains blocked until apply is available and the author selects the exact change.

## Stress-test real fault lines

Use one proposal per real unresolved trade-off.
Cover important fault lines without a fixed quota of invented dilemmas.
Each proposal contains a unique ID, title, concrete proposed change, quoted draft principle and both-sides reasoning.
Both accepting and resisting the change must be defensible.
Consider tempting scope expansions, principle collisions, slippery slopes and identity questions the draft leaves open.
Replace predictable or softball questions.

Use the shipped board template and stylesheet through the bundled command.
Keep its full draft, card stack and review mechanics.
Do not restyle the board or substitute another approval surface.
Run command help for the currently implemented syntax and supply the documented input format.
Do not load assets or script source as instructions.
The build command is available, but launch, verdict ingestion and resumed review remain unavailable until C10b.
Building HTML does not prove that a review or approval happened.
Preserve the draft and report missing runtime or interaction prerequisites as blocked.

## Revise from author verdicts

Collect verdicts only for the matching run, exact draft revision and known proposal IDs.
Reject stale, missing, duplicate or unknown verdict bindings instead of guessing their meaning.
Keep the previous draft and decisions for resume.
The agent interprets the author's reasoning and proposes a new semantic draft revision.
A verdict alone cannot mechanically rewrite arbitrary vision prose.
Only exact, reviewed structured edits can support a mechanical update.
Trace every proposed edit to its verdict with a changelog in the review reply.
Fold the reasoning into the vision prose so future reviewers can apply it without the board.
Do not copy hypothetical IDs, verdict labels or transcripts into VISION.md.

Iterate in batches until the author explicitly approves the current draft or ends the review.
Approval of interview goals or a stack does not approve generated VISION.md.
Silence and interrupted sessions never count as approval.
Do not approve on the author's behalf or soften proposals to please them.
An ended review without approval remains incomplete.

## Finish and existing companion files

Deliver the approved draft or delta for selected protected apply.
Confirm its purpose, commitments, boundaries and accept/resist tests stand alone.
If an existing companion answers file contains approved reasoning, propose folding missing reasoning into the vision and removing the companion and its pointers as selected protected changes.
Do not delete or migrate it directly while apply is unavailable.
Merge overlapping reasoning and keep the vision concise.
Board transcripts remain in scratch and never become project documents.

Before reporting vision review complete, verify:

- Each drafted commitment traces to actual evidence or confirmed author decisions.
- Every real unresolved fault line was tested with both sides fairly stated.
- Current verdicts are bound to the matching run, draft and proposal IDs.
- Every revision traces to author reasoning and preserves the previous draft.
- The author explicitly approved this revision.
- The approved vision is sufficient without an answers file or transcript.
