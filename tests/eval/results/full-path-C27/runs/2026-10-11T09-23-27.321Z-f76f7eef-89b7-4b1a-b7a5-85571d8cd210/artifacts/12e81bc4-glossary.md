# Pantry vocabulary

- Item: one named pantry supply.
- Needed: explicitly marked by the household as needing to buy, independent of stock.
- Shopping list: the items marked needed.
- Duplicate name: names equal after trimming and case-insensitive comparison; reuse the existing item.
- Variant: a supply distinguished by its full supplied name.
- Unsaved changes: session changes whose saving failed; reload retention is not assured.
