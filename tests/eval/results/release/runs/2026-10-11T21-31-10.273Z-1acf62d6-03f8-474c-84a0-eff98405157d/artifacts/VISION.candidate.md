# Reading queue

Status: approved by the owner.
Help one reader save articles and mark them read.
Success means a saved article can be found in the queue and marked read without losing its title or address.
Accounts, sharing and recommendations are outside the first version.
There are no open product or stack decisions.

Accept changes that let the reader export their own saved articles locally for personal backup, preserving title, address and read status.
Resist changes that turn personal backup into sharing or introduce accounts or recommendations.
This portability commitment does not authorize implementation or a stack change.
