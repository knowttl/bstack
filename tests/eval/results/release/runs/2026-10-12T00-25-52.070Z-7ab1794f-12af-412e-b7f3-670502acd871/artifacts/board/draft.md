# Reading queue

Status: approved by the owner.
Help one reader save articles and mark them read.
Success means a saved article can be found in the queue and marked read without losing its title or address.
Accounts, sharing and recommendations are outside the first version.
There are no open product or stack decisions.

## Contribution boundaries

Accept local export of the reader's own saved articles, preserving title, address and read status, for personal backup.
Resist changes that turn personal backup into sharing, accounts or recommendations.
This portability commitment does not approve an implementation or stack change.
